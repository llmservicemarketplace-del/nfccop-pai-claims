AI that fits the claim and computer

Choose frontier-model capability or fully private local AI.

AI helps read documents and suggest what photographs show. Estimate quantities and prices come from the structured catalog, measurement sources, court-record pricing, regional factors, inflation adjustment, and user-confirmed inputs.

Best overall capability

Frontier models

Connect a supported hosted AI provider using an API key. This option works immediately without a local model download and is often faster on computers with modest hardware.

  • Strong document understanding
  • Strong photo-description assistance
  • No large local model installation
  • Provider usage charges may apply
  • Submitted claim content goes to the selected provider
Maximum on-device privacy

Private AI

Run AI locally on the Windows computer. Claim content processed through the local model stays on the device, with no per-use model-provider cost.

  • Reads RAM and CPU capacity
  • Shows which included models fit
  • Marks the recommended model
  • Handles the one-time model download
  • Provides local runtime controls
Automatic setup: PAi Claims assesses the system, compares model requirements with the computer, recommends a fit, downloads the chosen model, and manages its local runtime. Model downloads are approximately 2–9 GB depending on the selection.

AI assists; the estimating engine calculates.

AI contribution

Read documents, assist with extraction, and suggest what damage photographs appear to show.

Quantity contribution

Use roof data, measurement reports, user-confirmed dimensions, estimate measurements, and assembly rules.

Price contribution

Use the repair catalog, public-court-record price anchors, regional factors, inflation adjustment, and sourced local overrides.

Choose based on the claim—not fear.

For many homeowners, a frontier model offers the best balance of convenience and capability. For sensitive files or users who prefer on-device processing, the automated private-AI option provides a practical alternative.
