Explainable outputs

Show the work behind the total.

PAi Claims is built to make an estimate and its supporting record easier to review by exposing the inputs, assumptions, sources, qualifications, and unresolved issues that matter.

What a reviewable output can communicate

  • Claim and property context entered by the user.
  • Provisional line items, descriptions, units, quantities, and totals.
  • Scope sources and how an item entered the estimate.
  • Catalog prices and disclosed local-price overrides.
  • Assumptions used to complete incomplete information.
  • Evidence references and file-integrity identifiers.
  • Items requiring additional review or documentation.
  • Qualifications explaining what the output does not establish.

Estimate reports

Present structured repair lines and calculated totals in a format suitable for detailed review.

Measurement reporting

Explain the measurements used by the estimating workflow so quantities can be checked.

Review requirements

Surface missing, ambiguous, unsupported, or otherwise unresolved inputs instead of burying them.

Review gates protect the workflow

A review gate is a prompt to stop and resolve an issue. It is not a certification that everything outside the gate is correct.

1

Source check

Is the file, quote, price, or observation identifiable and connected to the case?

2

Scope check

Is the repair operation supported by the documented condition and appropriate repair method?

3

Quantity check

Are dimensions, units, waste, counts, and area assumptions supportable?

4

Price check

Does the catalog or override reflect a source suitable for this location and time?

5

Limitation check

Does the output clearly distinguish estimating work from coverage, causation, legal, engineering, and claim-payment decisions?

From comparison to demand—and forward to PAi Legal

After the carrier estimate is imported, PAi Claims can surface scope and dollar differences, combine the current estimate with confirmed payments and policy information, and build a demand-letter draft. If the dispute remains unresolved, the same structured case can be exported for PAi Legal.
