NFCcop disaster-relief pathway

Ask whether free disaster-relief access applies.

NFCcop may make a free PAi Claims offer available in eligible disaster-relief circumstances. Availability, duration, verification, product access, and eligibility can change and should be confirmed before relying on the offer.

What this pathway should explain

Current availability

Whether the disaster-relief offer is active when the request is made.

Eligibility

What disaster, location, timing, or documentation requirements apply.

Access terms

Which version or access period is provided and what happens when that access ends.

Support boundaries

Whether the offer includes software access only or any additional education or assistance.

Privacy expectations

What information is needed to evaluate eligibility and how claim material should be protected.

No outcome promise

Free access does not mean a claim will be covered, increased, reopened, accepted, or paid.

Offer status: This draft page intentionally does not invent eligibility rules or promise that the offer is currently open. NFCcop should publish precise, current terms before activating an application or redemption flow.
