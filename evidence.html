Evidence-centered work

Keep the source material connected to the claim.

PAi Claims is designed to preserve original case material and make it easier to trace estimate and report work back to the documents, photographs, and review decisions that informed it.

What the evidence workflow covers

Document intake

Bring claim-related documents into the workspace, including estimate material and supporting records.

Original-file preservation

Keep original bytes available in case uploads while derived text or review material is added separately.

Text extraction

Make available text from supported documents and images easier to review, search, and compare. Extraction can be imperfect and must be checked.

Photo review inputs

Use photographs as evidence inputs and observations, while avoiding the claim that a photo alone proves dimensions, causation, coverage, or repair cost.

Source tracking

Connect scope and report work to available supporting sources instead of presenting conclusions without context.

Evidence integrity indicators

Evidence hashes can help identify the material associated with a case record. They do not independently prove authenticity, origin, or legal admissibility.

Not automatic proof: Extracted text, AI-assisted observations, file hashes, and imported documents still require human evaluation. PAi Claims does not establish that a loss is covered or that an item is owed.

What still requires a person

  • Confirm the file belongs to the correct claim and property.
  • Check OCR or extracted text against the original page.
  • Confirm what each photograph actually shows.
  • Separate observed damage from assumptions about cause.
  • Verify measurements with suitable field information.
  • Determine whether professional inspection is needed.
  • Review policy language and carrier decisions separately.
  • Decide whether evidence is sufficient for the intended use.
