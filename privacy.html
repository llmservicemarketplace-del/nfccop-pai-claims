Privacy and control

Understand where claim information goes.

Most homeowners can choose the AI experience that best matches their priorities. Frontier models generally provide the strongest document and photo assistance and send submitted claim content to the selected provider. For users who need maximum privacy, PAi Claims can configure AI to run on the Windows computer itself.

Privacy principles to verify in use

Frontier-model performance

Connect a supported hosted provider with an API key for immediate access to capable models, especially when the computer has modest hardware. Submitted claim content goes to that provider under its terms and usage charges.

Original material

Original imported bytes can be preserved alongside extracted text and review data, helping the user distinguish evidence from derived content.

Optional transfers

Phone or companion workflows should be treated as deliberate transfers. Confirm the paired device, network, recipient, and case before sending sensitive material.

Automatic private-AI setup

PAi Claims reads available RAM and CPU capacity, evaluates the included local-model choices, identifies which models fit, and marks the recommended option. The software handles the model download and local runtime controls.

Commerce

Microsoft Store access, licensing, and purchase activity are subject to Microsoft's systems and account controls.

User responsibility

Claim files may contain addresses, financial information, photographs, policy records, and other sensitive data. Use secure devices and authorized workflows.

Two valid choices: Many homeowners will prefer frontier-model capability and convenience. Private local AI is available for claims where keeping submitted content on the device is the higher priority. The estimating catalog—not the AI—supplies quantities and prices.

Before importing a real claim

  • Use a trusted Windows account and updated device.
  • Know where case data and exports will be stored.
  • Limit device access to authorized people.
  • Review backup and deletion procedures.
  • Confirm any companion device before pairing or transfer.
  • Review outside-service settings before using AI-assisted functions.
  • Avoid sharing reports more broadly than necessary.
  • Retain original documents according to applicable obligations.
