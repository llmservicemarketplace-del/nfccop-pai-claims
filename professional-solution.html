Professional claim software

A complete claim solution designed for adjusters.

PAi Claims gives adjusters and claim professionals one structured environment for field capture, evidence ingestion, roof and property measurements, scope development, location-aware estimating, carrier comparison, settlement review, reporting, demand preparation, and legal-ready case continuity.

One professional workspace across the life of the claim

Inspection and field capture

Record a narrated phone walkthrough, take photographs, import drone-supported material, use Google roof data, and ingest formal measurement reports.

Evidence and document intelligence

Preserve original files, extract available text, organize photographs and narration, track sources, and retain file-integrity identifiers.

Complete repair scope

Build a trade-by-trade repair scope with catalog line items, connected assemblies, measurements, waste, taxes, access work, detach/reset operations, and supporting notes.

Location-aware estimating

Use public-court-record price anchors, regional labor and material factors, inflation adjustment, and documented local quotes to form a traceable price basis.

Carrier comparison

Ingest the carrier estimate, compare scope and replacement-cost figures, identify missing or different operations, and update the professional estimate as new evidence arrives.

Settlement and dispute workflow

Track deductible, payments, depreciation treatment, and the supported amount in dispute; create reports and a reviewable demand-letter draft where appropriate.

Professional outputs

Produce printable estimates, measurement information, source explanations, assumptions, review requirements, report packages, and supporting enclosures.

PAi Legal continuity

When legal review becomes necessary, export a revision-matched, integrity-checked case graph and claim workspace that carries the existing evidence and item status forward.

Flexible AI architecture

Use frontier models for high-capability document and photo assistance or let PAi Claims assess the workstation and recommend a private local model.

Designed for professional judgment

PAi Claims does not replace the adjuster’s judgment. It gives that judgment a more complete evidence, calculation, and reporting system.

  • Sources remain connected to the work they support.
  • Measurements remain distinguishable from visual observations.
  • AI interpretations remain separate from estimating arithmetic.
  • Assumptions remain visible instead of silently becoming facts.
  • Changes can trigger estimate and demand-document refreshes.
  • Regional and inflation inputs remain explainable.
  • Unresolved information is carried forward for review.
  • Case continuity reduces repeated data entry between claim and legal workflows.

From first inspection to final professional package.

PAi Claims is designed to help an adjuster create the complete claim solution—not merely calculate a number.
