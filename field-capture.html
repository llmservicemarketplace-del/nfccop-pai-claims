Capture the loss where it happened

Walk, talk, photograph—and bring the inspection into the estimate.

PAi Field Recorder lets an adjuster, contractor, inspector, or policyholder narrate a walkthrough while taking pictures. PAi Claims imports the session, transcribes the audio locally, connects nearby narration with the corresponding photos, and turns the organized observations into claim and estimating inputs.

The phone walkthrough workflow

1

Record while walking the property

Speak naturally about rooms, materials, visible damage, access issues, and repair questions while taking photographs on the phone.

2

Import the field session

Bring in a PAi Field Recorder package or use an iPhone Voice Memos recording with the walkthrough photographs. Android and iPhone workflows are supported through their available import paths.

3

Transcribe on the computer

PAi Claims uses an on-device speech model to transcribe the narration offline. The software can download the speech model and show transcription progress.

4

Pair words with pictures

Audio timing and photo timestamps connect narration spoken near the shutter time with the relevant picture. General walkthrough narration remains available even when it is not assigned to one photo.

5

Build the observation record

Photos, narration, timing, transcription confidence, file hashes, and warnings become an organized field record inside the claim.

6

Use it in claim preparation

The organized observations feed document review, scope development, repair assemblies, measurements, and the provisional estimate without requiring the user to retype the entire inspection.

Roof intelligence from more than one source

Google roof and aerial data

With a Google Maps API key and Solar API access, PAi Claims can look up available building roof data from an address or coordinates and develop an aerial roof takeoff.

Drone imagery and measurements

Drone photographs and measured information can support roof shape, slope, component, damage, and quantity review, or replace an aerial assumption with a stronger measured source.

Measurement reports

Import roof measurement reports such as EagleView, Hover, GAF QuickMeasure, Roofr, or similar PDFs so squares, pitch, eaves, rakes, ridges, hips, valleys, and flashing can be tied to the report line they came from.

Intentional source hierarchy: Aerial data provides a fast starting point. Measurement reports, drone-supported measurements, and confirmed field dimensions can strengthen or replace the initial roof values. The system records the source so the estimate does not hide where a measurement came from.

Built-in integrity and review signals

  • Hashes imported field-session files and compares them with phone-recorded values.
  • Preserves the audio and original photographs with the claim record.
  • Records low transcription confidence instead of silently treating every word as certain.
  • Recognizes recording pauses caused by calls or alerts.
  • Separates photo-specific narration from general walkthrough narration.
  • Keeps spoken observations distinct from confirmed measurements and coverage positions.

From field capture to location-aware pricing

Once the loss is organized, PAi Claims combines the documented scope with measurement sources, court-record price anchors, regional factors, and inflation adjustment.
