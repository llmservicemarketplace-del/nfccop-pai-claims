What it is

Professional claim software for building the complete case.

PAi Claims is built for adjusters and claim professionals who need field evidence, estimating, comparison, settlement information, reports, and case continuity in one system. It supports professional judgment without pretending that software alone decides coverage or guarantees acceptance.

From first documentation to resolution—or legal handoff

One continuing record lets the homeowner prepare early, compare the carrier's work, request the supported difference, and carry the same organized case forward if the dispute does not resolve.

1

Document before the adjuster arrives

Capture photos, narration, measurements, roof data, documents, and the early loss condition in one claim workspace.

2

Build the preliminary homeowner estimate

Use repair assemblies, measurement sources, court-record price anchors, regional pricing, and inflation adjustment to create a rough estimate before the carrier's inspection is complete.

3

Import and compare the carrier estimate

Identify scope, quantity, price, payment, deductible, and depreciation differences in the same case.

4

Update the supported difference

Resolve conflicts, confirm new facts, add supporting material, and regenerate the estimate when the case changes.

5

Build and review the demand-letter draft

Use the estimate, policy, documents, payments, and amount in dispute; confirm flagged facts before deciding whether to send it.

6

Continue in PAi Legal when necessary

Export a revision-matched, integrity-checked case in the format PAi Legal reads so evidence and assumptions do not have to be reconstructed from scratch.
